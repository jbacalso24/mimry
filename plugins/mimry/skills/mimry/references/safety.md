# MIMRY safety

Good roots are focused repos, product folders, docs vaults, or curated active-work folders.

Bad roots:

- `/`
- a whole home directory
- `C:\`
- `C:\Users\you`
- system/config/cache folders
- dependency directories such as `node_modules`

Generated paths such as `.mimry/`, `.mimry/mimry-out/`, `.git/`, and dependency caches are support artifacts, not source fixes.
